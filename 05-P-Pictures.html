Step 2 · Others

Helping Someone See Their Strengths Picture

How to Apply It to Others When someone loses their cool, snaps at someone else, or hangs their head after a mistake, help them see their Strengths Picture. Don't lecture them; call out what they are capable of when they channel their heart and energy into helping the group move forward.
Real-Life Scenario: During a family move, your younger sister Mia is in charge of packing the kitchen. A box she packed breaks, and several dishes shatter. She snaps, "I can't do anything right!" and storms off to her room. Your parents are already stressed, and someone mutters, "Typical."

🎯 Try This Today: Switch On Your Alter Ego Once

  1. Pick one moment in the next 24 hours that usually makes you hold back—speaking up in a meeting, setting a boundary, making a phone call you've been avoiding.
  2. Right before it, use your Switch-On Signal and say your Alter Ego name in your head ("Okay, Anchor").
