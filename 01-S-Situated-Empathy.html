S — Situated Empathy

Honest Self-Check & The RESPECT Action Plan

Empathy is not soft or weak—it is a sharp awareness skill that starts with being honest about the pressure you are carrying right now, because your body doesn't lie. Once you can see your own stress clearly, you can step into other people's shoes using the RESPECT action plan so they feel safe rather than judged.

Step 1 · Yourself

The Honest Body & Stress Self-Check

How to Apply It to Yourself: Situated empathy starts by being completely honest about what you are dealing with right now instead of pretending to be a superhero who never feels pressure. If you feel nervous, frustrated, or scattered, that reaction makes total sense given what is happening in your life. Instead of beating yourself up for not being perfect, give yourself space to breathe, recharge, and reset from where you actually stand.

Step 2 · Others

The RESPECT Card Picker & Action Plan

How to Apply It to Others: When working or living alongside others, situated empathy means stepping into their shoes so they feel safe rather than judged. When people feel safe around you, they drop the fake mask and show up with real openness and confidence.
Real-life scenario: Jordan, a coworker on your team, has missed two deadlines this month. In today's meeting, they kept their eyes down, gave one-word answers, and left as soon as it ended. A few people are grumbling that Jordan "doesn't care anymore." You've noticed Jordan's shoulders are tense and they've been taking a lot of personal phone calls in the hallway.
