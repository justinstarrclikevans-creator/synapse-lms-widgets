Step 2 · Others

Help Someone Spark Their Own Why

How to Apply It to OthersWhen someone hits a slump, giving them a canned pep talk or telling them what they "should" do usually goes in one ear and out the other. Instead, ask thoughtful questions that spark their memory: a tough moment they handled well before (Aptitude), what they care about right now (Motive), and what drew them to this path in the first place (Enjoyment).

Step 4 · Your Summary

Your Inner Why Plan

🎯 Try This Today — Name It, Reset It, Cue It
  1. Morning: Read your One-Line Inner Why out loud once before you start your day.
  2. During the day: The first time something goes wrong, use your Physical Reset Routine within 5 seconds—shoulders down, one breath, your reset word.
  3. Before a demanding moment (a meeting, a hard conversation, a class): whisper your 3-Word Focus Cue to yourself.
  4. Evening: Ask one person in your life a single Aptitude, Motive, or Enjoyment question—and just listen to their answer.
